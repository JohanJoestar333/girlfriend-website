Optional: use Kikivoice (cloned voice)

Kikivoice can clone a short sample of your voice and turn text into an MP3. It has no API, so you use it on their site, then upload the file here.

  1. Open kikivoice.ai (free credits reset weekly).
  2. Upload 3–15 seconds of clean speech (no music/background noise).
  3. Paste the message you want spoken and generate audio.
  4. Download the MP3.
  5. Back here: Upload audio, optionally add text, then Send card.

For everyday notes, Record on this page is simpler — it’s really your voice, with no weekly limit.
